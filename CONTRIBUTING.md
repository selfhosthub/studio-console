# Contributing

Contributions are welcome. Before you open a pull request, read this, because submitting one has a condition attached.

## Copyright assignment

**By submitting a contribution you assign its copyright to the copyright holder.** This is Section 5 of the [LICENSE](LICENSE):

> Contributions to this project are assigned to the copyright holder and their successors and assigns. By submitting a contribution, you agree to this assignment and represent that you have the right to make the contribution.

Opening a pull request means you accept that assignment and that you have the right to make the contribution. If your employer owns your work, get their sign-off first.

Console is source-available, not open source. The right to read and modify it for your own use does not carry a right to redistribute it, and a merged contribution does not give you one.

## Before you open a PR

- Open an issue first for anything larger than a bug fix, so the approach can be agreed before you spend time on it.
- Keep the change focused. One concern per PR.
- Match the surrounding code. No new dependencies without discussion.

## Security

Do not report vulnerabilities through pull requests or public issues. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
